# Buy WebCodex a coffee ☕

[English](SPONSORSHIP.md) | [简体中文](SPONSORSHIP.zh-CN.md)

WebCodex is free and open source. If it has saved you time or made AI-assisted development easier, you can buy the maintainer a coffee to support ongoing development, testing, and maintenance.

**Sponsorship is entirely optional.** Every feature remains available without a donation. Stars, bug reports, sharing the project, and pull requests are equally appreciated.

## Buy me a coffee

If you want to support the maintainer personally, scan one of the QR codes with WeChat or Alipay and select an amount supported by your payment app. No advance contact is needed.

On a phone, you can open the [WeChat QR image](assets/sponsor/vx.png) or [Alipay QR image](assets/sponsor/zfb.png), save it, and use your payment app's scan-from-gallery option if available.

> These are voluntary tips sent directly to the maintainer, **not payments processed by GitHub**. Verify the recipient shown in your payment app before paying. Tips do not purchase software, support, or other services.

## What does your support help with?

Maintenance, cross-platform testing, builds and releases, and essential infrastructure costs. Donations do not buy roadmap priority, privileged access, or guaranteed merges.

## Presentation and independence

Donation information stays in the repository's README and this documentation—not in Desktop or Runtime popups. No third-party tracking is added for donation attribution. Support does not influence security decisions, reviews, release decisions, or project direction.

## Other ways to help

If you'd like to contribute test hardware, CI capacity, code-signing resources, or other infrastructure, feel free to open a [GitHub Issue](https://github.com/yyjeqhc/webcodex/issues) to discuss it. Never post credentials or sensitive information publicly.

Thank you to everyone who uses, shares, and contributes to WebCodex.
